What tickles your fancy?

Whether you prefer traditional masala dosa like you would find on the streets of Chennai or more modern fusion dishes like our paratha taco filled with pulled Chettinad chicken curry - we got you covered. Our menu constantly evolves and is naturally subject to availability, so sometimes we might have to make some last minute changes or more mouths than we could feed that day. But we always try to keep our menu page up to date, so check it out and see if there's anything that excites you today! (:
